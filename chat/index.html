How RPS works
  1. A trainer creates or reuses a scenario template and a scorecard template, optionally generated with the help of AI from uploaded Knowledge Base documents.
  2. The trainer pairs both templates into a simulation, sets the language, voice, difficulty, and due date, then assigns it to a trainee.
  3. The trainee starts the simulation and has a live conversation with an AI-powered customer persona for the configured time limit.
  4. Once complete, the AI generates a draft evaluation against the scorecard, with per-question scores and reasoning tied to the transcript.
  5. The assigned trainer reviews every AI-generated evaluation, can adjust any score, and finalizes feedback before the trainee sees it.
  6. The trainee reviews their results and recording, and can request a human review or report an AI issue at any time.
AI transparency & Gemini disclosure

RPS uses generative AI to power the customer persona you speak with, to transcribe and translate conversations, and to draft evaluation scores and reasoning. This is a practice simulation — the AI may make mistakes, misunderstand context, or produce an inaccurate assessment.

All AI-generated content in RPS is labelled with an AI Generated badge so it's always clear what came from the model versus a human trainer.

General-purpose AI model in use: RPS uses Google's Gemini family of models for conversation generation, transcription/translation, and evaluation drafting, together with ElevenLabs for multilingual voice synthesis. Model outputs are reviewed by a human trainer before being treated as final feedback.

You can request a human review of any evaluation, or report an AI issue, at any time from the Trainee Results screen.

Privacy & data retention

Simulation audio, transcripts, and evaluation data are stored to allow trainer review, trainee self-coaching, and campaign-level reporting. Knowledge Base documents uploaded by trainers are stored at the campaign level and used only to inform scenario and scorecard generation for that campaign.

Access to a trainee's simulation data is limited to that trainee, their assigned trainer, and campaign administrators. Recordings and transcripts can be exported by a trainer for coaching purposes.

Retention periods follow your organization's data retention policy; contact your campaign administrator for the specific schedule that applies to you.

FAQs
Can I exit a simulation partway through?
Yes. You can exit an in-progress simulation at any time from the pre-simulation or in-call screen. Exiting early may result in an incomplete evaluation.
What happens after I request a human review?
Your assigned trainer is notified immediately and will follow up on the specific evaluation you flagged. This does not change your recorded score until the trainer makes an adjustment.
What's the difference between "Report AI Issue" and "Request Human Review"?
Report AI Issue flags a technical or quality problem (e.g. a nonsensical AI response) to the RPS support team for investigation. Request Human Review asks your trainer to personally re-check your specific evaluation.
Is my simulation recording shared outside my campaign?
No. Recordings and transcripts are only visible to you, your assigned trainer, and your campaign's administrators.
Incident response workflow
Documented process from issue report through resolution, applying to both trainer- and trainee-submitted reports (Report AI Issue).
1
Report received
User submits a Report AI Issue with issue type, description, and simulation ID. Logged automatically with timestamp and reporter identity.
2
Triage (within 24h)
Support team reviews severity and category (score accuracy, voice/audio, safety, other) and assigns an owner.
3
Investigation
Owner reviews the flagged simulation transcript, AI output, and relevant model logs to determine root cause.
4
Resolution & corrective action
Score corrected if needed, trainee/trainer notified, and, where systemic, a fix or prompt/model adjustment is scheduled.
5
Closure & monthly reporting
Incident closed with a summary; aggregated trends feed into the monthly AI bias & accuracy review above.
